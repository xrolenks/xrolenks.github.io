How to play

Knock your rival's health to zero. Win two rounds to win the fight. Try each step on the dummy in Practice.

1. Move and guard

A D walk, W jump, S crouch. Tap a direction twice to dash.

Hold L to block. Standing blocks high attacks and overheads; crouching blocks lows.

2. Punch and kick

J punch, K kick. Add S for an uppercut or a leg sweep.

Jump in with J: an overhead that hits even if they crouch.

3. Strings

Press buttons one after another while your hits land, e.g. Kade's J J J. A miss stops the string.

A blocked last hit leaves you open, so don't throw it out carelessly.

4. Throws

Up close, press J + K together. It goes through blocks. Hold back to throw them behind you.

Being grabbed? Press J + K fast to break free, or crouch to duck it.

5. Special moves

↓ → + J throws your energy blast. Some fighters have more specials.

Do the motion right after a punch lands to cancel straight into it, e.g. J J then ↓ → + K for Kade's Fiery Rush.

6. Learn more

Every fighter's moves, strings and specials are in the Move list. Esc or P pauses; change keys in Controls.
